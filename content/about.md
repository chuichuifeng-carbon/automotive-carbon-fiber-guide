**This is CCF's supporting automotive carbon fiber buyer guide. Our official B2B website is [ccfrp.com](https://ccfrp.com/).** These resources help overseas buyers prepare requirements, evaluate samples and compare sourcing options. Current products, projects and quotations are handled on the main website.

## Who publishes the guide?

CHUICHUIFENG Carbon Fiber (CCF) is the international B2B identity of Guangzhou Chui Chui Feng Automotive Products Co., Ltd. (广州市吹吹风汽车用品有限公司). The head office is in Guangzhou; the production base is in Qingyuan, Guangdong, China. AMXNX is the consumer-facing aftermarket brand.

Use the [official company page](https://ccfrp.com/about/) as the starting point for verification. The [detailed company profile]({{< relref "posts/about-ccf-automotive-carbon-fiber-manufacturer.md" >}}) explains what buyers should reconfirm before ordering.

## How to use our advice

We publish from a supplier's perspective and have a commercial interest in introducing CCF. Apply the same verification questions to us that you would to another supplier. Educational examples are not quotations, test reports or product-specific promises.

- Compare the same vehicle, SKU, construction, quantity and delivery scope.
- Request test-fit records, samples and documents relevant to the exact requirement.
- Confirm estimates, commercial terms and remedies in writing.
- Obtain appropriate specialist review for customs, structural use and market requirements.

Updated dates indicate substantive content revisions. Product availability and commercial terms may change independently of an article's date.
