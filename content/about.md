# About CHUICHUIFENG Carbon Fiber

CHUICHUIFENG Carbon Fiber focuses on automotive carbon fiber manufacturing and supply for international automotive aftermarket businesses.

We work with distributors, wholesalers, performance shops, e-commerce sellers, aftermarket brands, and companies developing automotive carbon fiber products.

## Our Focus

Our business is centered around automotive carbon fiber products and B2B manufacturing support.

Our customers may need:

* Wholesale automotive carbon fiber parts
* Product sourcing
* Private label products
* OEM development
* ODM development
* Automotive e-commerce sourcing
* Product-line development

## Manufacturing Partner for Global Automotive Businesses

For B2B customers, a supplier needs to provide more than a product catalog.

Important considerations include:

* Product fitment
* Surface quality
* Product consistency
* Production planning
* Packaging
* Communication
* Lead time
* Long-term supply

We aim to support customers throughout the sourcing and manufacturing process.

## Our Approach

We believe automotive carbon fiber sourcing should begin with clear product requirements.

Before discussing production, we recommend confirming:

1. Vehicle application
2. Product specification
3. Quantity
4. Target market
5. Finish requirements
6. Packaging
7. Delivery requirements

This helps both sides establish a clearer manufacturing plan.
