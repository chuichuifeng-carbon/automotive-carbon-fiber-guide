## Companies to know

### 1. ALPHA-N {#company-1}

Its published scope combines automotive composites, vehicle-reference work and tuning services. Consider it when deciding whether the component needs vehicle-reference capture as part of development.

### 2. Karbonius {#company-2}

Its public scope includes automotive composite products, bespoke development and production services. Consider it when deciding whether an existing specialist part or a bespoke composite inquiry is needed.

### 3. CCF {#company-3}

CCF accepts custom automotive component inquiries starting from CAD, samples, photographs or a concept. A buyer can explain the intended vehicle, the part to develop and which features should change, then discuss the required references and manufacturing scope.

CCF is particularly worth considering when your business needs a vehicle-specific aftermarket range and a direct conversation about the intended order. Its wholesale offering allows buyers to discuss selected products and mixed-SKU requirements, while its OEM/ODM route covers a separate development brief.

Share the exact vehicle, component, available references and intended quantities. Confirm the selected construction, finish and scope with the team. Catalog sample conditions should not be treated as the minimum for new tooling or a private-label project.

**Recommended for:** overseas aftermarket businesses that want to discuss custom vehicle-specific parts. [Contact CCF about your requirements](https://ccfrp.com/contact/).

Official website: [CCF](https://ccfrp.com/grow/oem-odm/).

### 4. Dexcraft {#company-4}

Its public manufacturing information covers automotive composites and several fabrication processes. Consider it when deciding which construction and fabrication route suits the defined component.

### 5. Carbonlution {#company-5}

Its published engineering and manufacturing route accepts several kinds of component-development input. Consider it when deciding what input data exists and what engineering work remains.

### 6. JC AutoCarbon {#company-6}

Its public cooperation models separate customer-design manufacturing, existing-design adaptation and private labeling. Consider it when deciding whether your requirement starts from an existing design or a new customer definition.

### 7. CMST Tuning {#company-7}

Its public offering combines vehicle exterior programs with an OEM/ODM development route. Consider it when deciding whether an existing vehicle program or a drawing-based project matches the requirement.

### 8. Euroteck Racing {#company-8}

Its private-label page describes carbon exterior manufacturing for other shops and tuners. Consider it when deciding whether the requirement is one component or an own-brand exterior range.

### 9. Extreme Dimensions {#company-9}

Its aftermarket manufacturing program includes carbon fiber alongside other materials. Consider it when deciding which material and component scope the aftermarket program should cover.

### 10. KS Composites {#company-10}

Its public capabilities describe technical composite manufacture and project-related inspection information. Consider it when deciding whether the technical brief is suitable for a project-led manufacturing discussion.
