**Do not order BMW M3/M4 carbon fiber parts by model name alone.** Specify the chassis, body style, production date, bumper configuration and exact component. Compatibility must be confirmed for the SKU and revision being supplied; a broad “M3/M4” listing is not sufficient evidence.

## Identify the vehicle before selecting a part

G80 refers to the M3 sedan, G82 to the M4 coupe and G83 to the M4 convertible. These identifiers help narrow an inquiry; they do not make body panels, spoilers or trim interchangeable.

Ask the customer for vehicle details and photos of the interfaces the part will contact. When necessary, use VIN-based configuration information through an appropriate vehicle reference or dealer. Market-specific model years and production changes should be checked rather than inferred from registration year alone.

## Part-by-part sourcing questions

| Product | What to verify before quoting | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|
| Front lip / splitter | Bumper shape, underside mounts, ground clearance and kit content | Test-fit photos showing mounting interfaces |
| Rear diffuser | Bumper variant, exhaust arrangement and adjacent equipment | Rear and underside installation views |
| Side skirts | Body style, length, mounting method and jacking-point access | Left/right fitment and mounting instructions |
| Trunk spoiler | Deck-lid shape, body style and adhesive/drilling method | Contact-surface photos on the exact application |
| Mirror covers | Housing, clips, options and replacement-versus-overlay design | Backside and installed comparison |
| Hood / trunk replacement | Latches, hinges, supports and relevant vehicle systems | Application-specific installation and engineering evidence |
| Interior trim | Existing layout, controls, fixing points and equipment | Matching interface and clearance photos |

Ask the supplier to list any drilling, trimming, bonding or additional hardware. Avoid selling a part as “direct fit” unless the documented installation method supports that description.

## Pre-LCI and LCI: confirm the affected interface

A facelift label is a useful question, not a complete compatibility rule. Lighting, interior and exterior updates need not occur together or affect every component. Request the production-date and market scope actually checked for the SKU.

Do not assume all earlier and later front lips differ, or that all engine-bay parts carry over. Record verified applications, explicit exclusions and untested configurations. For a new application, agree a physical test-fit before a larger order.

## Compare construction without inventing performance claims

Specify prepreg/wet process, full-carbon or hybrid backing, finish, replacement versus overlay, finished weight and hardware. See the [dry versus wet carbon guide]({{< relref "posts/dry-vs-wet-carbon-fiber.md" >}}).

Avoid generic weight-saving tables: a new lip or overlay may add weight rather than replace an equivalent original assembly. Publish a saving only when equivalent configurations have actually been weighed. Track-use capability, aero loads and road approval require evidence beyond visible weave or a process label.

## Copyable RFQ for a BMW product shortlist

> Vehicle: [G80/G82/G83, production date, market, relevant options]. Products: [separate SKUs]. Please confirm supported applications, test-fit evidence, construction, finish, installation method, supplied hardware, sample availability, quantity price breaks, packed dimensions and lead time. Identify any unverified fitment and development work separately.

For a kit, list every component and exclusion. Keep body-style-specific components separate instead of assuming one kit fits sedan, coupe and convertible.

## Sample approval and resale records

1. Check the actual vehicle configuration against the quotation.
2. Have an installer dry-fit the sample using the proposed instructions.
3. Photograph mounts, clearances and adjacent sensors/equipment.
4. Record finish acceptance, packaging and hardware completeness.
5. Release the agreed revision and keep the evidence with the SKU.

Use the [sample evaluation checklist]({{< relref "posts/carbon-fiber-sample-evaluation.md" >}}) and maintain the same application limits in your product listings and customer quotations.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do G80 and G82 parts always interchange?

No. Some products may share applications, but compatibility must be verified component by component. Body style and mounting surfaces matter.

### Does a carbon fiber part improve performance?

Not automatically. Cosmetic appearance, weight change and aerodynamic performance are separate claims requiring different evidence.

### Where can I check CCF's current applications?

Use the [official vehicle directory on ccfrp.com](https://ccfrp.com/vehicles/) to prepare a shortlist, then ask CCF to confirm the exact SKU, availability and fitment scope for your order.
