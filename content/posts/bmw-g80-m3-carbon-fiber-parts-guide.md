A G80 M3 owner asks your shop for a carbon fiber package. The reference photograph shows a front lip, side skirts, a rear diffuser and a high rear wing. It looks like one purchase. In practice, you are pricing several products, several mounting interfaces and possibly an exhaust change.

**Choose BMW G80 M3 carbon fiber parts around the customer's intended build and the car's current configuration.** A front lip, side-skirt pair and trunk spoiler can form a styling proposal without turning the job into a replacement-panel project. A different diffuser, wing or hood brings its own fitment, installation and support work. Agree that scope before offering a package price.

This guide is for tuning shops and distributors selecting G80 products. It covers what belongs in the quote, where similar-looking references diverge and what to inspect before committing to wholesale stock.

## G80 M3: identify the car behind the inquiry

G80 identifies the M3 sedan. G82 is the M4 coupe, G83 the M4 convertible and G81 the M3 Touring. A grouped M3/M4 catalog helps locate designs; it does not establish that every side skirt, trunk panel or spoiler fits every body.

For the initial shortlist, get the production details, current bumper photographs and any relevant modifications. A repaired bumper, an aftermarket exhaust or an existing lip can change the job even when the registration says the expected model year. For LCI applications, ask which production range was checked for the exact part rather than assuming a facelift affects every component in the same way.

Start with [CCF's BMW model catalogs](https://ccfrp.com/vehicles/bmw/) and select the G80/G82 catalog. Keep the individual product reference with your inquiry. ## Which G80 carbon fiber parts belong in the build?

The following is a buying map, not a sales ranking. Choose the rows that match the customer's request; there is no need to turn every inquiry into a complete exterior conversion.

| Product | What the customer is choosing | What can change the quote |
|---|---|---|
| Front lip / splitter | Outline, projection and section design | Bumper interface, hardware, packed size and installation |
| Side skirts / sill extensions | Side profile and visible width | Body-specific variant, pair contents and underside attachment |
| Rear diffuser | Fin design and rear-bumper treatment | Exhaust layout, surround, separate outer pieces and any light |
| Trunk spoiler or rear wing | Low-profile deck-lid styling or a raised assembly | Contact surface, supports, drilling and mounting requirements |
| Mirror caps and grille | Smaller visible carbon accents | Overlay/replacement structure, housing and equipment compatibility |
| Hood or trunk lid | Replacement body panel and styling | Latches, hinges, transferred equipment, alignment and packaging |

BMW's own [M Performance overview for the M3 and M4](https://www.bmw-m.com/en/topics/magazine-article-pool/bmw-m-performance-parts-bmw-m3-m4.html) illustrates how broad the carbon accessory range can be. It describes BMW products. Its installation and performance statements do not establish what an independently supplied aftermarket part includes or does.

## Front lips: select the profile, then price the complete assembly

The front lip is where a styling photograph can most easily hide the purchasing details. Look at the center profile, corner shape, forward projection and how the component returns underneath the bumper. Those affect the installed appearance and the workshop's access to the fixings.

CCF's [three-piece CSL-style G80/G82 front lip](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-g82-carbon-fiber-front-lip-three-piece-csl-style/) is a concrete catalog reference: it shows separate outer sections and a connecting section. The quoted set should identify all supplied sections and the finish. An accent shown in a photograph should not silently become part of the order.

A three-piece design also raises a useful after-sales question: can a damaged section be reordered in the same revision? The fact that the lip comes apart does not answer it. Before promising inexpensive corner replacement, obtain the actual supply option.

For the customer's car, review existing trim and any previous holes. If the vehicle is lowered, discuss the intended use and assess clearance on that configuration. Neither a stock-height catalog photograph nor a style name tells your shop how it will negotiate the customer's driveway.

Our [G80/G82 front-lip buying comparison]({{< relref "posts/carbon-fiber-quote-hardware-scope.md" >}}) goes further into construction, mounting evidence and carton costs. Use that detail once the customer has narrowed the outline.

## Side skirts: keep the sedan variant visible on the order

Write G80 M3 sedan on the purchase line, with the confirmed supplier code. A generic “M3/M4 skirt pair” is too easy to confuse with another body variant at ordering or receiving.

For the [MP-style G80/G82/G83 side-skirt reference](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-g82-g83-carbon-fiber-side-skirts-mp-style/), CCF asks buyers to verify body-specific codes, length and attachment layout. The grouped heading is a way to find the style, not a reason to pool all variants into one warehouse SKU.

Also establish whether the selected product extends below the existing sill, covers a surface or replaces a specified component. That tells the installer which original parts remain and which attachment information is needed. The words “side skirt” alone do not define the work.

A pair should be itemized as vehicle-left plus vehicle-right where that is the agreed supply unit. Inspect both together, including their ends and visible edges. During the mounting review, include the underside and any relevant service or lifting access. A clean side-on image cannot show those areas.

## Rear diffusers: the exhaust chooses the branch of the catalog

Ask whether the owner is keeping the current exhaust. If it has aftermarket tips or a planned conversion, obtain that reference before selecting the diffuser.

The [G80/G82 MP-style rear diffuser](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-g82-carbon-fiber-rear-diffuser-mp-style/) should be assessed by its opening arrangement and supplied sections. Identify any separate surround, outer pieces, clips and brackets in the quotation. A completed rear-end photograph may show all of them together even when they are separate purchases.

A center-exit design needs a compatible exhaust combination. It is not simply another fin pattern for an unchanged car. Tip size, position and projection matter even when two systems have a broadly similar layout. Have the installer and suppliers review the actual combination and its relevant heat-clearance requirements.

For a customer retaining the exhaust, keep the quote focused on a diffuser with supported application to that setup. A conversion can be offered as a separate proposal with its own components and labor. Our [G80/G82 diffuser fitment guide]({{< relref "posts/carbon-fiber-prototype-release-scope.md" >}}) covers what to do when the opening or bumper interface does not match during the sample review.

## A trunk spoiler and a raised wing are different jobs

A lower-profile spoiler follows a contact area on the deck lid. A raised wing adds a support and mounting arrangement that must be considered as part of the purchase. Neither can be selected by width alone.

For a G80 customer, CCF has a [G80/G82 MP-style rear-wing reference](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-g82-carbon-fiber-rear-wing-mp-style/). Obtain the actual application, supplied supports and hardware, mounting method and any required modifications for the chosen version. A shared listing title is insufficient evidence that the trunk interfaces are interchangeable.

Discuss how the product attaches before the customer approves the job. If the installation requires holes, brackets or other changes, those affect the decision as much as the carbon finish. Have the appropriate installer review the mounting requirements; appearance alone does not establish suitability for aerodynamic loads.

Avoid assigning a downforce figure or handling improvement to a catalog-style wing without supporting evidence for that product and configuration. A shop can explain the styling result accurately without inventing a performance benefit.

## Carbon hoods and trunk lids need a separate quotation

A replacement panel is a different scope from adding exterior trim. CCF lists a [G80 M3 AC-style carbon fiber hood](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-m3-carbon-fiber-hood-ac-style/) and a [G80 M3 CSL-style trunk lid](https://ccfrp.com/bmw-g80-m3-carbon-fiber-trunk-lid-csl-style/). Treat each as its own installation review.

For a hood, the review includes hinge and latch arrangements, underside layout, engine-bay clearance, support requirements and the selected vent design. For a trunk lid, it includes alignment, closure and the transfer or accommodation of relevant lighting, camera and trim components. The supplier should identify which components are supplied and which come from the car.

Do not quote a percentage weight saving from the word “carbon.” Compare equivalent assemblies with the same weighing scope if weight is a purchasing requirement. A cosmetic carbon surface does not by itself establish the complete panel construction.

Packaging also changes at this size. Get the proposed packed dimensions, gross weight and protection method before finalizing freight. A hood can materially change the shipment plan; its transport share should not be estimated as though it were another pair of mirror caps.

## Dry carbon, weave and gloss: keep three decisions separate

The visible pattern, surface finish and manufacturing construction answer different questions. A 2x2 twill face does not tell you what backing is present. Gloss does not establish wet layup, and matte does not establish prepreg construction.

CCF states a dry-carbon focus in its BMW range, while individual product specifications still require confirmation. Ask for the construction of the selected SKU, including any carrier, backing or bonded mounting feature. Use our [dry versus wet carbon explanation]({{< relref "posts/dry-vs-wet-carbon-fiber.md" >}}) when comparing offers that use different process descriptions.

For a multi-part build, compare the proposed visible finish across the lip, skirts and rear pieces. Similar weave names do not guarantee an exact visual match. An approved sample or agreed photographs are more useful than a promise that every carbon product looks the same.

## Installation and sample review before the bulk order

Have the workshop review the installation documents for the selected references. Photograph the delivered labels, component contents and attachment sides before permanent fitting. Then record what the sample actually required on the identified vehicle: retained parts, additional hardware, adjustments and any unresolved issue.

If the lip has to be forced into position or a diffuser interferes with the exhaust, send evidence before trimming away the original interface. The supplier needs to see the mismatch to assess whether the reference, vehicle condition or supplied part is responsible.

For resale, keep those findings with the SKU. An attractive final installation photograph is useful marketing material, but it does not tell the next installer how much work went into the job.

## Get the G80 catalog and a product-specific wholesale price

A useful first inquiry is a short product list: G80 vehicle details, selected references, finish, quantity per line and destination. Include the bumper and exhaust photographs where they affect the selection. For a package, list the components instead of asking for “everything in this photo.”

Send that list through [CCF wholesale supply](https://ccfrp.com/grow/wholesale/) to request the catalog references and pricing. Ask for sample terms, supplied hardware, packing and current delivery scope for those products. MOQ and availability belong to the actual quotation; one published number should not be applied to every lip, wing and replacement panel.

For stock, begin with the verified applications your own customers request. Keep optional styles and larger panel projects available by quotation until demand and installation support justify carrying them. That produces a G80 range your staff can sell accurately, instead of a large catalog of parts they cannot distinguish.

## G80 M3 buyer questions

### Will G82 M4 carbon fiber parts fit a G80 M3?

Some supplier references may cover both, but the application must be established for each component. Side skirts, trunk interfaces and other body-specific areas deserve particular attention. Use the confirmed code for the G80 order.

### Are MP-style carbon parts genuine BMW M Performance parts?

A styling description does not establish original-equipment origin. Identify the manufacturer and quoted product. BMW's information describes its own accessories and does not validate an aftermarket reference.

### Which parts should a tuning shop order first?

Start with the customer's requested job and the products your installer can evaluate. A lip, skirt pair and spoiler can be assessed as separate selections; a diffuser conversion, wing or replacement panel may need a larger installation scope. There is no universal best-selling G80 package established by this guide.
