**Approve a carbon fiber sample against a written specification, not a general impression.** Record the SKU, revision, vehicle configuration, inspection method, acceptance criteria and unresolved issues. A sample can demonstrate what was supplied; it cannot guarantee that every production part will match it.

## Before the sample ships

Ask for the component's construction, replacement-versus-overlay design, mounting method, supplied hardware, finish, sample status and verified application. State whether the sample must use production tooling, production materials and production packaging. Identify any differences from the proposed bulk order.

Agree who pays for samples, freight, test fitting and revisions. Do not assume sample approval includes tooling ownership, product certification or authorization to manufacture the full order.

## Six checks to document

| Check | Method and evidence | Approval basis |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Photograph label, SKU, revision, left/right marking and supplied kit | Matches the order and application |
| Construction | Review written material/process statement; weigh the complete assembly | Matches the specified construction and declared measurement scope |
| Fitment | Dry-fit on the stated vehicle with an installer; photograph interfaces | Agreed gaps, clearances and installation method |
| Appearance | Inspect under consistent lighting; photograph show faces and edges | Approved finish standard and defect examples |
| Function | Check mounts, adjacent equipment, moving parts and instructions | Meets the defined application requirements |
| Packaging | Record unpacking, contact points, protection, dimensions and damage | Appropriate for the planned delivery route |

Use component-specific tolerances agreed before inspection. A single universal gap limit, coating thickness or gloss value is not appropriate for every part.

## Fitment checklist for the installer

- [ ] Vehicle model, generation, body style, production date and relevant options recorded.
- [ ] Bumper, mirror housing, exhaust and existing modifications identified where relevant.
- [ ] Mounting points align using the agreed installation method.
- [ ] Sensor fields, cameras, lamps, cooling paths and moving parts remain unobstructed.
- [ ] Gaps and left/right alignment documented at named reference points.
- [ ] Required drilling, bonding, trimming and hardware disclosed before approval.
- [ ] Installation photos and open issues linked to the exact sample revision.

Avoid modifying a disputed sample before recording its condition and agreeing the next action. Modifications can obscure the cause of a mismatch.

## What a visual inspection cannot prove

Weave appearance, hand flexing and informal tapping do not establish structural strength, absence of delamination or prepreg construction. Cosmetic inspection is separate from engineering validation. If a hood, wing or other assembly has safety-relevant loads or interfaces, agree the required engineering review and testing with a qualified specialist.

Do not apply solvents, cut samples or perform coating adhesion tests without an agreed method and permission. Destructive testing should be planned, with a separate sample where appropriate.

## Release, conditional approval or hold

Use one of three explicit outcomes:

1. **Approved for the defined next stage:** all release conditions are met.
2. **Conditional approval:** list each concession, permitted quantity and required correction; do not use an informal “looks fine.”
3. **On hold:** record failures and request correction and re-evaluation before production release.

Retain an identified reference sample or agreed photo/measurement record. See CCF's [prototype approval checklist](https://ccfrp.com/carbon-fiber-prototype-approval-checklist/) for documenting revision and sign-off scope.

## How to check the bulk order

Agree a pre-shipment inspection plan separately: lot definition, random selection, inspection level, defect classes, acceptance limits and action after a failed inspection. An acceptance sampling result is not a guarantee that every uninspected part conforms. For critical features, specify the required verification explicitly.

Quality-system certificates and supplier assessments have defined scopes. Check issuer, validity, company, site and product applicability; they do not replace inspection of the purchased SKU.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can photos replace a physical sample?

Photos help screen appearance and documentation, but cannot establish actual fitment, assembly weight or installation behavior. Use a physical sample when those factors affect your purchasing decision.

### What if the sample passes but the shipment differs?

Compare the batch with the approved revision and reference record, document the discrepancy, isolate affected stock and follow the agreed claims process. Preserve packaging and inspection evidence.

### Should production start before sample approval?

Only under an explicit, documented release defining what may proceed and who accepts the associated risk. Otherwise keep sample approval and production authorization as separate gates.
